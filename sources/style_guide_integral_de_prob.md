# Integrals, differential equations, probability: style notes

Own notes for building Khmer data. Short terms and stock phrases only; no exercise or solution is reproduced.

Chapter order, from the Grade 12 High Level answer-book list: 4 definite integrals, 5 differential equations, 6 probability distributions. Basic Level chapter 4 is also integrals.

**Read:** sample pages of Kim Sokun's High Level answer books for chapters 4, 5 and 6 (pages 3, 14, 18, 28; 3, 20; 3, 12, 20, 30). One author, a teacher's model solutions, not student scripts. No Khmer teacher has checked these notes.

## Layout seen on the pages
- Exercise number in Khmer digits, parts `ក. ខ. គ. ឃ.`, heading `ដំណោះស្រាយ`, each part repeated, then the working.
- Short lines beginning `គេបាន`, `យើងបាន`, `គេមាន`, `តាង`, `ដោយ`, `នាំឱ្យ`, `$\Rightarrow$`. Closing `ដូចនេះ` with a boxed result.
- Evaluated integral written with a bar and limits. New limits under substitution shown in a two-line brace.
- Constant noted as `($c$ ថេរ)`. Second order uses $A$, $B$.
