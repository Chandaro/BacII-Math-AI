# Terms for conics and space geometry

These are the words used by `gen/steps/conic.py` and `gen/steps/space.py`.
[SEEN] means the word was read on a Khmer page named here. [UNVERIFIED] means no page was read for it. A Khmer maths teacher should check every [UNVERIFIED] row before the data is used for students.

Spelling follows the rest of the dataset (ចំណុច, ចម្ងាយ, ទម្រង់, តម្រុយ), not the older spellings on some pages (ចំនុច, ចំងាយ, ទំរង់, កំនុំ).
